How did Psychology Research Begin?

There's arguments on what really started the acknowledgement of Psychology that has led to its modern development. Some argue that it began in the 1500 BCE. An Ancient Egyptian manuscript called called the Ebers Papyrus mentions what we call Clinical Depreseeion. However, the connection between this and Psycholopgy was only seen in the 11th century by a Persian physician named Avicenna.

Another group argues that it truly began with Willhelm Wundt ("Father of Modern Psychology") when he established the first experimental psychology lab in 1879.

Additionally, a third group, belives that modern American psychology emerged from the philosophy that was praacticed by John Locke and Thomas Reid. They proposed ideas of empriricism, which proposed that all knowledge must come from experience and emphasized the role of the human senses in the minds ability to acquire knowledge.
