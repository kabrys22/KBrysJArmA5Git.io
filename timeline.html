Psychological Breakthroughs

This is only some of the significant psychological breakthroughs depicted throughout history

  • 1879: Willhelm Wundt establishes the first formal labratory for psychological study
  • 1883: The first formal US psychology labratory is established
  • 1887: G. Stanley Hall founded the American Journal of Psychology in the United States.
  • 1890: William James publishes the Principles of Psychology in the United States
  • 1892: The American Psychological Association (APA) is organized by G. Stanlet Hall
  • 1898: Edward Thorndike in the UnitedStates publishes Animal Intelligence. Leads to later development of Operant Conditioning.
  • 1900:Sigmund Freud in Austria and England publishes The Interpretaion of Dreams. Freud is considered the founder of psychoanalysis.
  • 1906: Ivan Pavlov in Russia publishes his first studies on classical conditioning
  • 1920:John Watson and Rosalie Raynor conducted the controversial "Little Albert" experiment.
  • 1927: Anna Freud in Austria and England, the sixth and youngest child of Sigmund Freud, publishes Introduction to the Technique of Child Analysis. Freud developed the field of child psychoanalysis
  • 1943:Starked Hathaway in the United States and J. Charnlet McKinley in the United States publishes the Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory (MMPI)
  • 1952: The American Psychiatric Association publishes the first Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM).
  • 1957: B.F. Skinner in the United States publishes Schedules of Reinforcement
  • 1979: Elizabeth Loftus in the United States publishes Eyewitness Testimony. Her contributions lead to advances in the field of memory, misinformation, and eyewitness memory.
