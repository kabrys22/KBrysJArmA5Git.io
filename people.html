Famous Psychologists

William James (1890)

  • William James is known as the Father of American psychology.
  • He wrote "The Principles of Psychology" (1890)
  • William James is listed as number 14 on the American Psychological Association's list of the the 100 most eminent psychologists of the 20th century

Sigmund Freud (1890s-1930s)

  • Sigmund Freud founded psychoanalysis, focusing on unconcious thoughts and bringing them in conscious awareness. He had controversial theoeries, but made important contributions to psychology
  • Known for his idea that not all mental illnesses have physiological causes

Ivan Pavlov (1890s-1900s)

  • Ivan Pavlov laid the foundation for behaviorism and was famous for his classical conditioning which originated not due to psychological research but on his study of digestive methods.

John B. Watson (1913)

  • John B. Watson is known most famously during the Rise of Behaviorism. He argued that psychology should focus on observable behavior rather than internal mental processes
  • Known for his "Little Albert" experiment
